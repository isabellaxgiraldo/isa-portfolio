How I work
Standards I don't bend on.
01 — Quotes

Verbatim, timestamped

Direct quotes stay exact, anchored to the timestamp they came from. No paraphrasing a quote, no invented language.

02 — Sources

Citations that hold up

E-E-A-T and credible sourcing, checked against the original. If a claim can't be verified, it doesn't ship.

03 — Format

Native to the channel

Copy is built for the platform it lives on — its length, its constraints, its rhythm — not pasted in from somewhere else.

04 — Language

Bilingual by default

Spanish and English, moving between the two without losing meaning, tone, or structure along the way.
